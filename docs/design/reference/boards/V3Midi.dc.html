How mappings work
Learn — click, then press the footswitch or move the pedal. Click the action to change what a control does, ✕ to remove it. Footswitches act once per press, also while Encore is in the background. What Stomp 3 switches and which knob Expression 1 moves is set per patch: right-click a block or a knob in Edit.
